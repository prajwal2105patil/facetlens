## Why these are reported but not merged

Deduplicating the catalogue is not this system's call. A facet that looks redundant here may be a deliberate distinction in the scoring instrument it came from. The pipeline therefore surfaces the pairs for a catalogue owner to adjudicate and leaves every row in place.

The practical consequence is that retrieval can return several near-identical facets in one top-K, spending scoring budget on what is effectively the same question. Collapsing clusters before batching is listed as future work in README.
